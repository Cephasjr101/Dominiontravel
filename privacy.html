Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

Almost nothing. No accounts, no database. Search forms open third-party booking sites or WhatsApp on your own device — your details never pass through our servers.

Stored on your device

  • Your cookie-consent choice (browser localStorage).
  • Cookieless analytics (if accepted) — no personal profiles, never sold.

Third parties

When you search or book, these providers process your data under their own policies: Skyscanner, Travelpayouts/Aviasales/Hotellook, Booking.com, Viator (TripAdvisor), and WhatsApp (Meta). Embedded widgets may set their own cookies — you choose whether they load.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is usually nothing to delete. Clear this site’s localStorage to reset your consent choice.
