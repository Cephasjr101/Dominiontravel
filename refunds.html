Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Service fees

Consultation/processing fees are refundable if work hasn’t started; non-refundable once processing begins.

Flights, hotels, cars & tours

Refunds and changes follow the fare/ticket rules of the airline or provider. We always confirm the rules before you pay. Many basic fares are non-refundable — we’ll flag that up front.

Transfers

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup; within 24 hours a partial fee may apply.
